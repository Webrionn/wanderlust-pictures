LATEST ACHIEVEMENT

Best Short Film (3rd Place) – “Blessed Rat” | University of Peradeniya

We are thrilled to share that Blessed Rat has won 3rd Place in the Open Category at Let My Country Awake 2026, organized by the Literary Subcommittee of the Students’ Union, University of Peradeniya! This is a deeply meaningful milestone for our team, validating our hard work and fueling our passion as we continue our journey in filmmaking. A huge thank you to everyone who supported us along the way. Here’s to many more stories to tell!
