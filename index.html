THRILLER/DRAMA2026

Blessed Rat

A short film about a university boy trapped in a time loop, reliving the same events over and over...

“Better to be myself than a winner in the rat race.”

In a world where everyone is running to get ahead, chasing success, and repeating the same routines every day, we often forget what it means to truly live.

Blessed Rat tells the story of a boy trapped in an endless cycle of work and repetition, living the same day over and over again. Like many of us caught in the rat race, he becomes so consumed by his daily routine that life begins to feel like a loop with no way out. Yet, there is something special about him — a blessing that makes his story different.

Through his journey, the film explores the pressure of modern life, the pursuit of success, and the quiet struggle to break free from a cycle that society has taught us to accept. It invites us to question whether winning the rat race is truly worth it, or whether the real victory lies in finding the courage to be ourselves.

Because sometimes, the greatest blessing is not winning the race, but realizing that we were never meant to run it.

THRILLER2025

WITHIN

A thriller following a young girl as she struggles to uncover the truth before it's too late...

“Is she trapped? you can decide it.”

A young girl finds herself trapped inside a house, caught in a terrifying nightmare that seems to have no end. Desperate to escape, she struggles to understand what is happening around her. The house feels strangely familiar, yet something about it is deeply unsettling. Every room holds an eerie silence, every corner seems to conceal something unknown, and the feeling that something is terribly wrong grows stronger with every passing moment.

Just when she believes she has finally escaped the nightmare, she wakes up, only to discover that something still isn't right. The same surroundings, the same unsettling atmosphere, and the same inexplicable events begin to repeat themselves. Each awakening brings her closer to questioning her own reality, as she becomes trapped in an endless cycle of dreams and false awakenings. What initially seems like a frightening dream slowly transforms into something far more disturbing, leaving her unable to distinguish between what is real and what exists only in her mind.

As fear takes hold and confusion deepens, she desperately searches for a way out, wondering whether she is still dreaming or trapped in a reality she cannot comprehend. With every failed attempt to wake up, the boundaries between dreams and reality grow thinner, turning her familiar surroundings into a place of uncertainty and terror.

But what if the real nightmare isn't being trapped in a dream, but believing you've finally woken up?

A psychological thriller that explores the mysterious world of dreams, the terror of false awakenings, and the fragile boundary between reality and illusion, leaving one haunting question behind: How can you escape a nightmare when you can no longer tell whether you're awake?

CRIME/THRILLER2025

The Unmarked Date

A crime thriller about two girls who commit a crime and try to hide the truth...

"In just minutes, everything can change."

A gripping crime thriller following two girls haunted by a crime from their past. Believing they have left their darkest secrets behind, they attempt to move forward with their lives, hoping that time will bury the truth and erase the consequences of their actions. However, the past has a way of returning when least expected, and no secret can remain hidden forever. Their sense of security begins to fade as the memories of what happened continue to haunt them, leaving them trapped between the lives they want to live and the truth they desperately want to escape.

Their fragile peace is shattered when the killer unexpectedly returns to their house, turning their familiar surroundings into a place of fear and uncertainty. As tension rises and the threat draws closer, the two girls are forced to confront the consequences of their past while struggling to protect themselves from what lies ahead. With trust becoming uncertain and their darkest secrets threatening to surface, every moment brings them closer to a truth they can no longer avoid. Because no matter how deeply you bury the past, some secrets always find their way back.

ROMANCE/SHORT FILM2025

Another Year

A romantic 4 minute short film about unspoken love and the pain of waiting for someone who may never feel the same way.

“Another Year Without Love”

Some people leave our lives, but the memories they leave behind stay with us. Another Year Without Love follows a young man trying to move forward after losing the girl he once loved. As time passes and another year goes by, he finds himself caught between the memories of what they shared and the reality of living without her. He hasn't found someone new, nor has he completely let go of the past. While the world around him continues to move forward, a part of him remains where their story ended. Sometimes, the hardest part of a breakup isn't losing someone; it's learning to live with the emptiness they leave behind.

PSYCHOLOGICAL THRILLER2024

In His Mind

A psychological thriller short film about a boy living alone in a house who feels like someone is watching him.

Sometimes, the scariest feeling isn't being alone. It's knowing you might not be.

A young boy living alone begins to experience strange and unexplainable events inside his own home. What once felt like a familiar and peaceful place slowly turns into something unsettling. Strange noises echo through the rooms, objects seem out of place, and an overwhelming feeling of being watched follows him wherever he goes. He tries to make sense of what is happening, but every attempt to find an explanation only leaves him with more questions. Is someone secretly watching him? Is there something hiding within the walls of his house, or is there something far more disturbing that he cannot understand? As fear begins to take over his everyday life, the boundary between reality and the unknown starts to blur. In a place where he should feel safest, he finds himself questioning everything around him.

SHATTERED SILENCE
DRAMA2024

Shattered Silence

Our first short film, following a boy trying to move on after a breakup while living through his usual daily routine.

Sometimes, a stranger's message arrives when you're still trying to forget someone else.

Our very first short film is here! A young boy tries to move on with his life after a painful breakup, finding comfort in his usual routine and spending his days working from home. As time passes, his life continues in the same quiet and predictable way, with little to distract him from the emptiness left behind. However, everything begins to change when he unexpectedly receives a message from a stranger. What starts as an ordinary conversation soon becomes something that draws him in, interrupting his routine and raising questions about who is on the other side of the screen. As he continues communicating with the stranger, his ordinary days take an unexpected turn, leading him into an experience he never anticipated.
